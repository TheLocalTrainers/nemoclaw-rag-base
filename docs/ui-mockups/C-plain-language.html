What's wrong
Critical The patient is taking a medicine the trial doesn't allow A new prescription clashes with the study drug and may be harming the liver.

Four days ago another doctor prescribed ketoconazole (an antifungal) for a toenail infection. The trial prohibits this class of medicine because it makes the body hold on to far more of the study drug — an estimated 3.5× more than intended. That extra exposure is a likely reason the liver tests below are so high.
