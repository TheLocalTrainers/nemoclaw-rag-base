# CLINICAL STUDY PROTOCOL: ONCO-2026-X88

**Protocol Title:** A Phase 2/3, Multi-Center, Randomized, Open-Label Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Nexavatinib (NEX-882) in Patients with Refractory Metastatic Colorectal Cancer  
**Protocol Number:** ONCO-2026-X88  
**Investigational Product:** Nexavatinib (NEX-882, oral kinase inhibitor)  
**Sponsor:** BioSovereign Therapeutics Inc.  
**Version / Date:** Version 3.1 / January 15, 2026  

---

## 1. Study Objectives and Endpoints

### 1.1 Primary Objective
To evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) according to RECIST v1.1 criteria as assessed by Blinded Independent Central Review (BICR).

### 1.2 Key Secondary Objectives
- Overall Survival (OS)
- Objective Response Rate (ORR)
- Safety and tolerability profile evaluated by NCI CTCAE v5.0
- Pharmacokinetics (PK) profile and exposure-safety relationships

---

## 4. Patient Eligibility and Screening

### 4.1 Key Inclusion Criteria
1. Histologically confirmed metastatic colorectal adenocarcinoma with progression on standard therapy.
2. Age >= 18 years at the time of signing informed consent.
3. ECOG Performance Status of 0 or 1.
4. Adequate organ function within 14 days prior to Cycle 1 Day 1:
   - Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC) >= 1,500 / uL
   - Platelets >= 100,000 / uL
   - Hemoglobin >= 9.0 g/dL
   - Total Bilirubin <= 1.5 x Upper Limit of Normal (ULN)
   - AST and ALT <= 2.5 x ULN (or <= 5.0 x ULN in the presence of documented liver metastases)
   - Serum Creatinine <= 1.5 x ULN or calculated eGFR >= 50 mL/min/1.73m2

---

## 5. Concomitant Medications and Prohibited Therapies

### 5.1 Permitted Concomitant Medications
Supportive medications including anti-emetics, analgesics, antidiarrheals, and replacement therapies are permitted according to institutional standards, provided they do not conflict with Section 5.2.

### 5.2 Prohibited Concomitant Medications (Important Protocol Deviations)
Co-administration of the following drug classes is strictly prohibited during the study period and for 14 days prior to Cycle 1 Day 1:

1. **Section 5.2.1: Potent and Moderate CYP3A4 Inhibitors**  
   Nexavatinib is predominantly metabolized by hepatic CYP3A4. Strong inhibitors significantly increase systemic drug exposure (AUC > 3.5-fold), substantially elevating the risk of severe transaminitis, QT prolongation, and acute bone marrow suppression.  
   *Prohibited examples include:* **ketoconazole**, **itraconazole**, **posaconazole**, **clarithromycin**, **ritonavir**, **cobicistat**, and **grapefruit juice**.
   
2. **Section 5.2.2: Potent CYP3A4 Inducers**  
   Strong inducers decrease Nexavatinib plasma concentrations to sub-therapeutic levels.  
   *Prohibited examples include:* **rifampin**, **carbamazepine**, **phenytoin**, and **St. John's Wort**.

3. **Section 5.2.3: Therapeutic Anticoagulants with High Bleeding Risk**  
   Concomitant systemic therapeutic anticoagulation with **warfarin** or high-dose direct oral anticoagulants (**apixaban**, **rivaroxaban**) is prohibited due to additive bleeding risks. Low-molecular-weight heparin (LMWH) for venous thromboembolism prophylaxis is permitted.

## 6. Dose Modifications and Toxicity Management

### 6.1 General Toxicity Principles
All toxicities must be graded according to the National Cancer Institute Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (NCI CTCAE) v5.0.

### 6.2 Hepatic Toxicity Management (ALT / AST Elevations)
Liver function tests (LFTs) including ALT, AST, total bilirubin, and alkaline phosphatase must be performed according to the schedule of assessments.
- **CTCAE Grade 1 (ALT/AST > ULN to 3.0 x ULN):** Maintain current Nexavatinib dose; re-check LFTs weekly.
- **CTCAE Grade 2 (ALT/AST > 3.0 to 5.0 x ULN):** Maintain current dose if asymptomatic; repeat LFT within 72 hours. If persistent > 7 days, withhold Nexavatinib until resolution to Grade <= 1.
- **CTCAE Grade 3 (ALT/AST > 5.0 to 20.0 x ULN):** **MANDATORY DOSE HOLD.** Withhold Nexavatinib immediately. Evaluate for viral hepatitis and concomitant hepatotoxins. If ALT/AST recovers to Grade <= 1 within 14 days, resume at one dose reduction level (150mg daily). If recovery requires > 14 days, permanently discontinue.
- **CTCAE Grade 4 (ALT/AST > 20.0 x ULN):** Permanently discontinue study drug.

## 8. Schedule of Assessments and Visit Windows

### 8.1 Visit Schedule and Allowable Windows
All scheduled clinic visits, blood collections, and radiographic tumor assessments must occur within the allowable windows specified below. Visits occurring outside these windows constitute an **Important Protocol Deviation (IPD)** that must be documented and reported:

| Visit Identifier | Target Study Day | Allowable Window | Key Required Assessments |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Screening** | Days -28 to Day -1 | Within 28 days prior to C1D1 | Consent, baseline labs, CT/MRI scan, ECOG |
| **Cycle 1 Day 1 (C1D1)** | Day 1 | Day 1 (no window) | Pre-dose labs, vitals, drug dispensing |
| **Cycle 1 Day 14 (C1D14)** | Day 14 | **Day 14 +/- 2 days (Days 12 to 16)** | Hematology, CMP (ALT/AST/Creatinine), vitals, AE review |
| **Cycle 1 Day 28 (C1D28)** | Day 28 | **Day 28 +/- 2 days (Days 26 to 30)** | Hematology, CMP, PK blood draw, pill count |
| **Cycle 2 Day 1 (C2D1)** | Day 29 | **Day 29 +/- 2 days (Days 27 to 31)** | Pre-dose labs, new cycle dispensing |
| **Efficacy Tumor Scan (Week 8)** | Day 56 | **Day 56 +/- 5 days (Days 51 to 61)** | Contrast-enhanced CT chest/abdomen/pelvis (RECIST 1.1) |
| **Efficacy Tumor Scan (Week 16)** | Day 112 | **Day 112 +/- 5 days (Days 107 to 117)** | Contrast-enhanced CT scan (RECIST 1.1) |

*Note on Visit Window Non-Compliance:* Any blood draw, safety assessment, or scan conducted outside the allowable window must be logged as a Protocol Deviation in the EDC within 5 working days.

## 9. Adverse Event and Serious Adverse Event Reporting

### 9.1 Definitions and Investigator Responsibilities (21 CFR 312.64)
The Principal Investigator is responsible for recording all adverse events occurring after informed consent. Any event meeting regulatory criteria for seriousness (death, life-threatening, inpatient hospitalization, persistent disability) must be reported to the Sponsor within **24 hours** of site awareness.

### 9.2 Expedited Regulatory Reporting (21 CFR 312.32)
The Sponsor must report any unexpected fatal or life-threatening suspected adverse reaction to regulatory agencies (FDA / EMA) within **7 calendar days**, and all other serious unexpected suspected adverse reactions (SUSARs) within **15 calendar days**.
